Korg Drumlogue Free Plugins

Free and experimental plugins for Korg Drumlogue, developed by CrystalMatrix, also known as Francisco Javier Balandra Sánchez.

Korg Drumlogue SDK Plugins

The following free plugins have been developed exclusively for the Korg Drumlogue hardware by Francisco Javier Balandra Sánchez (CrystalMatrix).

This repository is focused on the development of virtual instruments and audio effects for the Korg Drumlogue drum machine. All plugins have undergone extensive stability and performance testing directly on the hardware, including the implementation of an experimental Channel Strip.

The creative development process uses the free applications Pure Data and PlugData, together with the heavy.lib library, which provides the essential components required to convert Pure Data projects into the sdklogue format while offering excellent compatibility with Korg Drumlogue.

If you are interested in developing synthesizers and audio effects for Korg Drumlogue, it is recommended that you download the following free tools from their official websites:

1. Pure Data
   https://puredata.info

2. PlugData
   https://plugdata.org/download

3. heavy.lib (Wasted Audio)
   https://github.com/Wasted-Audio/heavylib

The plugins included in this repository have been developed by Francisco Javier Balandra Sánchez (CrystalMatrix), a producer and engineer with releases on the UK record labels EML Recordings and Lazzey Recordings. Every plugin has been subjected to extensive stability and performance testing at CrystalMatrix Studio.

In addition, part of the research and development behind this project has been carried out in collaboration with academic research activities at the National Autonomous University of Mexico (UNAM), with the objective of studying and emulating the behavior of a wide range of professional equalizers and audio processors.
